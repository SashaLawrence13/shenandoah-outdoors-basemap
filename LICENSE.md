`contours/` and `dem/` are derived from the USGS 3D Elevation Program
(3DEP) 1/3 arc-second DEM, a U.S. Government work in the public domain
(credit: "Elevation: USGS 3DEP"). `satellite.json` and `usgs-topo.json`
point at the USGS National Map's public-domain tile services
(`basemap.nationalmap.gov`); no USGS tiles are stored here.

release, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`glyphs/OFL.txt`).
